WM Technology, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Irvine, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--November 9, 2026-- WM Technology, Inc. (“WM Technology” or the “Company”) (OTC: MAPS), a leading marketplace and technology solutions provider to the cannabis industry, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“During the third quarter, we saw decreases in revenue and Adjusted EBITDA, as well as increased cash utilization,” said Susan Echard, Chief Financial Officer of WM Technology. “Revenue was primarily impacted by client payment difficulties. A significant portion of our operating expenses and cash spend went toward increased marketing spend, while the remainder primarily covered one-time expenses, including a securities litigation settlement and severance costs associated with our workforce reduction in the third quarter.”
“We have continued to focus on collections and stepped up our efforts to terminate clients when they are late with payments. While this had an impact on our top line during the quarter, we made this deliberate decision with the long-term health of our business in mind. We expect some of the clients who churned or decreased their spend during the quarter to return because of the importance of our platform to their businesses. Though we continue to see softness in challenged markets like Michigan, Colorado, and Oklahoma due to heavy regulation and price compression, our marketing momentum in these regions remains strong, and we believe the revenue will follow.”
“Optically, our third-quarter results reflect near-term pressures, but operationally, it was a successful quarter that we believe has laid the foundation for future growth,” said Doug Francis, Chief Executive Officer of WM Technology.
“Improvements to our digital marketing strategies in the third quarter presented us with opportunities to attract more consumers to the platform, and so we meaningfully increased our user acquisition budget. These strategies have been successful at driving key elements of our value proposition to clients, and we believe that this will lead to revenue growth. Additionally, we are launching a new sponsored ad product for brands this month. We anticipate this product will contribute to revenue shortly after launch.”
“As we look toward 2027, our corporate governance and strategic footprint are evolving to match our ambitions. Earlier this year, we voluntarily delisted from Nasdaq and announced our intention to deregister as an SEC reporting company. We expect that the transition away from public reporting will reduce our administrative overhead and compliance costs, and more importantly, grant us the operating latitude to execute our long-term strategy.”
“We believe this regulatory and structural freedom will directly support our strategic expansion deeper into the cannabis supply chain. Following our Nasdaq delisting, we established a clear roadmap for our growth strategy. We are being highly disciplined and targeting best-in-class assets that we believe will generate positive cash flow at reasonable multiples. We have identified some potential targets who we think may fit this profile and we plan to continue to evaluate other potential strategic transactions and opportunities.”
“We are playing the long game. We believe that the foundational work we completed in the third quarter—cleaning up our client base, optimizing our user acquisition costs, and finalizing our strategic expansion infrastructure—positions us incredibly well for the next phase of our growth.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenues for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $38.5 million as compared to $42.2 million in the prior year period. The overall decrease in revenues from the prior year period was primarily driven by decreases in revenues across core markets, reflecting a challenging operating environment for certain of our customers who have faced margin compression and cash flow constraints affecting spending.
◦Average monthly paying clients(1) of 4,899 was down from 5,221 from the prior year period, largely due to churn in more established markets, partially offset by new client acquisitions across certain developing markets.
1

◦Average monthly revenues per paying client(2) decreased to $2,618 from $2,693 in the prior year period, due to spend declines in established markets driven by continued industry challenges, such as price deflation and ongoing consolidation, partially offset by the churn of lower-spending clients, which raised the average across our remaining clients.
•Net loss was $5.9 million as compared to net income of $3.6 million in the prior year.
•Adjusted EBITDA(3) was $0.8 million as compared to $7.6 million in the prior year.
•Total shares outstanding across Class A and Class V Common Stock were 161.1 million as of September 30, 2026.
•Cash and cash equivalents totaled $51.0 million as of September 30, 2026, as compared to $62.4 million as of December 31, 2025.

About WM Technology
Founded in 2008, and headquartered in Irvine, California, WM Technology, Inc. operates a leading online cannabis marketplace for consumers together with a comprehensive set of commerce and compliant advertising solutions for cannabis businesses, which are sold to both storefront locations and delivery operators (“retailers”) and brands in the legalized cannabis markets in states and territories of the United States. WM Technology holds a strong belief in the power of cannabis and the importance of enabling safe, legal access to consumers worldwide.
Over the past 18 years, the Weedmaps marketplace has become a premier destination for cannabis consumers to discover and browse cannabis-related products, access daily dispensary deals, order ahead for pick-up and delivery by participating retailers (where applicable) and learn about the plant. The Company also offers commerce and compliant advertising solutions designed to help cannabis retailers and brands reach consumers, create business efficiency, and manage industry-specific compliance needs.
The Company is committed to advocating for full U.S. legalization, industry-wide social equity, and continued education about the plant through key partnerships and cannabis subject matter experts. Visit us at www.weedmaps.com.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Our financial statements, including net income, are prepared in accordance with principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”).
To provide investors with additional information regarding our financial results, we have disclosed EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA, both of which are non-GAAP financial measures that we calculate as net income (loss) before interest, taxes and depreciation and amortization expense in the case of EBITDA and further adjusted to exclude stock-based compensation, change in fair value of warrant liability, legal costs and other non-recurring charges, reduction in force expense, loss contingency, sale of domains, change in the TRA liability and other non-cash, unusual and/or infrequent costs in the case of Adjusted EBITDA. Below we have provided a reconciliation of net income (the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure) to EBITDA; and from EBITDA to Adjusted EBITDA.
We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because these metrics are key measures used by our management to evaluate our operating performance, generate future operating plans and make strategic decisions regarding the allocation of investment capacity. Accordingly, we believe that EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA provide useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating our operating results in the same manner as our management.
Each of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider any of these non-GAAP financial measures in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are as follows:

•although depreciation and amortization are non-cash charges, the assets being depreciated and amortized may have to be replaced in the future, and EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect cash capital expenditure requirements for such replacements or for new capital expenditure requirements;
•EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, our working capital needs; and
•EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect tax payments that may represent a reduction in cash available to us.
