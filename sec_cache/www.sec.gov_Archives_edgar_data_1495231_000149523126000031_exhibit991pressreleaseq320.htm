IZEA Reports Q3 2026 Revenue of $6.2 million, 94% increase in Managed Services Bookings
Enterprise Relationships Remain Strong Amid Market Uncertainties

ORLANDO, Fla. (November 12, 2026) - IZEA Worldwide, Inc. (NASDAQ: IZEA), a leading influencer marketing company that makes Creator Economy solutions for marketers, reported its financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Q3 2026 Financial Summary Compared to Q3 2025
•Revenue was $6.2 million, a decrease of $1.9 million, or 23%, from the prior-year period. While revenue from our three largest customers grew 21%, complemented by contributions from new customers, these gains were more than offset by lower revenues from other enterprise accounts, and the continued run-off of non-core small and mid-sized business ("SMB"), which contributed $1.2 million in the prior year
•Managed Services bookings totaled $7.0 million, an increase of $3.4 million, or 94%, from the prior-year period, and 54% sequentially from the second quarter. The improvement reflected stronger booking activity across multiple enterprise relationships, with a broader group of customers making substantial commitments during the quarter
•Total costs and expenses decreased 16% to $7.0 million, compared to $8.4 million, reflecting continued proactive cost management toward a more efficient structure aligned with our enterprise-focused business model
•Net loss totaled $0.4 million, or $(0.03) per share, compared to net income of $0.1 million, or $0.01 per share
•Adjusted EBITDA* for the quarter was $(0.2) million, compared to $0.4 million
•Cash and equivalents totaled $46.4 million as of September 30, 2026, with no long-term debt

Q4 2026 Financial Outlook
For the fourth quarter of 2026, management is providing a revenue outlook to give investors greater visibility into how recent bookings are expected to convert to revenue. The Company expects:
•Revenue between $7.5 million and $8.1 million; comparative quarterly growth between 24% and 34%
This outlook primarily reflects contracted bookings and expected campaign timing and assumes a limited contribution from customer opportunities not yet under contract. The Company has not established a practice of providing regular financial guidance and undertakes no obligation to update this outlook.

Q3 2026 Highlights
•IZEA grew its top three enterprise accounts faster than the market, increasing its share of creator marketing spend within these accounts and reinforcing the strength of its strategic client relationships.
•IZEA won new business from major entertainment and technology leaders, including Disney-ABC, HBO Max, Hulu and ASUS.
•IZEA executed high-visibility creator campaigns including Nestlé Vital Proteins at the US Open, Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, and Ravensburger’s Oh My Pigeons! launch.
•IZEA managed 100% of new campaigns through ZED, the proprietary AI-powered creator marketing operations platform, enhancing creator intelligence, brand safety, and campaign execution for enterprise customers.

Q3 2026 Financial Results
Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.2 million, a decrease of $1.9 million, or 23%, compared to $8.1 million in the prior year period. Enterprise customer performance was mixed, with revenue from our three largest customers increasing 21% year over year, alongside contributions from new enterprise relationships. This growth was outweighed by lower revenue from other established enterprise accounts and the continued impact of our strategic shift away from non-core SMB customers, which contributed approximately $1.2 million in the prior-year quarter. With this transition substantially


complete, the Company remains focused on acquiring new enterprise customers and expanding existing relationships.

Cost of revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.9 million, a decrease of $0.3 million, or 6%, compared to the prior-year period. The decrease was primarily attributable to lower campaign activity. Cost of revenue declined more slowly than revenue, reflecting comparatively lower margins from a large enterprise customer and internal delivery labor costs that did not decline proportionately with revenue.

Costs and expenses, excluding the cost of revenue, totaled $3.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $1.1 million, or 26%, compared to the prior-year period, reflecting continued efforts to align the Company's cost structure with its enterprise-focused business model. Sales and marketing expense decreased 33% to $0.7 million, primarily due to lower personnel costs. General and administrative expense declined 25% to $2.3 million, driven by reduced headcount and lower anticipated incentive compensation.

Net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.4 million, or $(0.03) per share, as compared to net income of $0.1 million, or $0.01 per share in the third quarter of 2025, based on 17.3 million and 17.1 million average shares outstanding, respectively.

Adjusted EBITDA (as defined below, a non-GAAP measure management uses as a proxy for operating cash flow) totaled $(0.2) million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $0.4 million in the comparative period.

As of September 30, 2026, our cash and cash equivalents totaled $46.4 million with no outstanding long-term debt.

We previously announced our commitment to repurchase up to $10.0 million of our stock in the open market, subject to certain restrictions. Through September 30, 2026, we have purchased a total of 908,798 shares, investing $2.6 million under the repurchase program. We purchased 250,583 shares in the third quarter with a total cost of $0.8 million.

Use of Key Metrics and Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Managed Services Bookings is a key metric representing total sales orders received during a period, net of cancellations and refunds. Contracts vary by customer and scope, ranging from custom content projects to integrated marketing campaigns, and generally extend from several months up to a year. Managed Services Bookings provide a useful measure of overall demand but are not necessarily predictive of quarterly revenue, as the timing of revenue recognition varies with contract size, complexity, and customer arrangements. Certain customers enter into annual spend commitments that establish a defined budget for services to be performed throughout the year, while others engage the Company for specific campaigns or deliverables. These differing contract structures may influence the timing and distribution of bookings and related revenue. The Company uses this metric to evaluate customer and market trends, to plan operational staffing, and to inform product development initiatives.


"Adjusted EBITDA" is a non-GAAP financial measure under the Securities and Exchange Commission rules. EBITDA is commonly defined as "earnings before interest income and expense, taxes, depreciation, and amortization." IZEA defines “Adjusted EBITDA” as earnings or loss before interest expense, interest income, taxes, depreciation and amortization, non-cash stock-based compensation, gain or loss on asset disposals or impairment, and certain other unusual or non-cash income and expense items such as gains or losses on settlement of liabilities and exchanges, and changes in the fair value of derivatives, if applicable. We believe that Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors as it primarily excludes non-cash and non-operating transactions, and it provides consistency to facilitate period-to-period comparisons.

Not all companies calculate bookings and Adjusted EBITDA in the same manner. These metrics and financial measures, as presented by IZEA, may not be comparable to those presented by other companies. Moreover, these metrics and financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. You should not consider them in isolation or as a substitute for an analysis of our results of operations or, with respect to non-GAAP financial measures, as reported under GAAP.
