ITG, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Record third quarter revenue and Adjusted EBITDA exceed outlook; 2026 outlook1 updated to include revenue growth of approximately 37% and Adjusted EBITDA growth of approximately 37%
THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS
•Revenue increased 49% year-over-year to $461.5 million
•Net loss attributable to ITG, Inc. of $6.1 million
•Adjusted EBITDA2 increased 64% year-over-year to $64.5 million
•Free Cash Flow2 of $62.8 million versus $26.2 million in Q3 2025
•NTM Backlog3 of $1,539 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $1,209 million as of September 30, 2025 and $1,517 million as of June 30, 2026, supporting strong revenue visibility
•Strong new order activity, including broadband fiber deployment awards with multiple new customers
•Record levels in the quarter for Revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and NTM Backlog
FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., November 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – ITG, Inc. (Nasdaq: ITG) (“ITG" or "the Company”), a leading provider of end-to-end services to the digital infrastructure industry, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the continued strength of the business and our ability to execute efficiently for customers even as activity increased substantially in the period,” said Andy Parrott, Chief Executive Officer of ITG. “Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA reached record levels and exceeded the outlook we provided last quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin also improved both year-over-year and sequentially, reflecting higher activity levels, improved asset utilization and solid execution by our operating teams.”
“Demand remains broadly healthy across our communications and digital infrastructure markets, with particular strength in broadband wireline infrastructure deployment. We continue to expand our work with both newer and long-standing customers and leverage growth opportunities created by significant customer investment in broadband, network upgrades and data center connectivity. Our broad turnkey services, national operating model and ability to support customers across both new installation and full lifecycle services position us well as a leading service provider for our customers.”
“We enter the fourth quarter with substantial revenue visibility and are actively exploring opportunities for partnerships with new customers, including several new wins confirmed in September. Our priorities remain focused on executing for our customers, converting awarded work into profitable revenue, improving cash conversion and focusing on disciplined capital allocation. Our updated outlook reflects our confidence in the business and our expectations for continued profitable growth,” Mr. Parrott concluded.

ABOUT ITG, INC.
ITG is a leading provider of end-to-end services to the communications and digital infrastructure industries throughout the United States. ITG supports the planning, design, construction, operation, maintenance, and expansion of broadband, wireless, data center, utility, and civil infrastructure. With a workforce operating across 49 states, ITG is positioned to build and maintain the digital backbone powering our future.
