Mount Logan Capital Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Year-to-date FRE1 of $3.3 million and SRE1 of $8.2 million produced Segment Income1 of $11.5 million, up 21%, or $2.0 million, year-over-year

Insurance Solutions SRE of $2.7 million, up $1.2 million year-over-year, and down $0.3 million from the prior quarter alongside Asset Management FRE of $0.5 million, down $2.2 million year-over-year, and $0.9 million from the prior quarter

Two strategic transactions were completed during the third quarter–SOFIX’s acquisition of assets from the Yieldstreet Alternative Income Fund, and the merger of the Alternative Credit Income Fund with BC Partners Lending Corporation–which are expected to add $6.5 million of annualized FRE1 on a run-rate basis

During the quarter, Ability Insurance Company received a B+ Financial Strength Rating from AM Best and officially launched its initial suite of multi-year guaranteed annuity ("MYGA") products under the ReliAbility™ brand

After quarter end, Mount Logan signed a definitive agreement to succeed Alternative Fund Advisors, LLC as investment adviser to the AFA Asset Based Lending Fund, which is expected to bring Mount Logan’s interval fund assets under management to approximately $460 million.2

Third Quarter Financial and Business Highlights
•Total revenue for the Asset Management segment was $2.0 million for the quarter, a decrease of $0.8 million, or 27% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Asset Management revenue excludes $1.7 million of intercompany management fees earned from managing the assets of Ability Insurance Company ("Ability"), which remained relatively flat from the same 2025 period.
•Fee-Related Earnings (“FRE”) for the Asset Management segment were $0.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, down $2.2 million compared to $2.7 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•Total net investment income for the Insurance Solutions segment including net investment income of consolidated variable interest entities ("VIEs") was $20.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $0.5 million, or 2%, compared to the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the funds withheld assets under reinsurance contracts and modified coinsurance ("Modco") arrangements, the Insurance Solutions segment’s net investment income was $14.2 million, an increase of $0.2 million, or 1%, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Achieved a 7.3%3 yield on the insurance investment portfolio for the third quarter of 2026. Excluding the funds withheld under reinsurance contracts and modified coinsurance, the yield was 7.8%.
•Spread-Related Earnings (“SRE”) for the Insurance Solutions segment was $2.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.5 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•Mount Logan's total assets under management ("AUM") was $1.8 billion as of September 30, 2026 and is inclusive of Ability’s assets managed by Mount Logan. Ability's assets excluding intangible assets and the funds withheld assets under reinsurance contracts and Modco, were $691.2 million as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of $96.3 million from the third quarter of 2025. As of September 30, 2026, the Insurance Solutions segment held approximately $1.0 billion of total investment assets, a decrease of $62.7 million from the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in AUM is primarily attributable to the SSL reinsurance recapture transaction that occurred during the quarter. Including Modco assets, Ability's total assets managed by Mount Logan is $863 million, an increase of $27 million compared to the third quarter of 2025.

Subsequent Events and Strategic Updates
•On October 20, 2026, Mount Logan Management LLC entered into a definitive agreement to become investment adviser to the AFA Asset Based Lending Fund, a roughly $230 million interval fund, which will be renamed the Mount Logan Asset Based Lending Fund upon closing (the “Proposed AFA Transaction”). The transaction will bring the Company’s interval fund assets to approximately $460 million and is expected to be immediately accretive to FRE upon closing, which is expected in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the fund's shareholder and regulatory approvals.
•On October 27, 2026, BC Partners Group Holdings Limited and certain affiliates (“BC Partners”), a long-standing shareholder and strategic partner of Mount Logan, announced a trading plan to purchase up to $1.5 million of Mount Logan common stock in the open market over the next 12 months. The plan was adopted under Rules 10b5-1 and 10b-18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and commenced on October 23, 2026, with purchases expected to continue through September 22, 2027. The purchases will be executed through an independent broker-dealer, Mount Logan is not a counterparty to the plan, and BC Partners currently holds approximately 2% of the Company’s outstanding common stock.

About Mount Logan Capital Inc.

Mount Logan Capital Inc. is an integrated alternative asset management and insurance solutions firm focused on generating durable, fee-based revenue and long-term value creation. The Company leverages differentiated investment strategies alongside permanent insurance capital to deliver attractive, risk-adjusted returns across market cycles.

Through its subsidiaries, Mount Logan Management LLC and Ability Insurance Company, Mount Logan manages and invests across private and public credit markets in North America and operates an insurance platform that provides long-duration liabilities to support its credit investment strategies. This integrated platform is designed to provide stable earnings, downside protection, and a low risk of principal impairment through the credit cycle.

As of September 30, 2026, Mount Logan Capital had over $1.8 billion in assets under management.
