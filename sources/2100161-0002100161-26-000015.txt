Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results
New York, November 16, 2026 -- Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust Inc. (NYSE: BXDC) today reported its third-quarter 2026 results. Net income for the quarter was $15.6 million, and net income per share was $0.15. Funds from Operations (“FFO”) per share, and adjusted FFO (“AFFO”) per share were $0.15 and $0.17, respectively.
Nick Pell, Chief Executive Officer and President said, “Today marks an important milestone for BXDC with our inaugural investment in thirteen newly constructed data centers across two campuses through a sale-leaseback with Amazon. We believe this transaction delivers highly compelling risk-adjusted returns and establishes a strong foundation for long-term shareholder value creation. Importantly, it highlights our ability to source unique investment opportunities as well as the evolution of our investible universe, as the hyperscaler sale-leaseback channel doubles our total addressable market. We believe BXDC is exceptionally well-positioned to capitalize on this growing opportunity set.”
Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust issued a transaction overview presentation, which can be viewed at www.bxdc.com. An updated company presentation may also be viewed on the website.

About Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust
Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust (NYSE: BXDC) is a recently listed company focused on acquiring and owning mission-critical data center assets that power the modern digital economy. BXDC targets newly-constructed, income-generating, stabilized data center properties leased to investment-grade hyperscale tenants on long-term contracts. Our investment strategy is designed to generate stable, long-term cash flows and deliver current income to shareholders, with growth potential through contractual rent escalations and accretive acquisition opportunities. BXDC is externally managed by an affiliate of Blackstone (NYSE: BX), the world’s largest alternative asset manager and the largest financial investor in data center and digital infrastructure assets globally. Further information is available at www.bxdc.com.
About Blackstone
Blackstone is the world’s largest alternative asset manager. Blackstone seeks to deliver compelling returns for institutional and individual investors by strengthening the companies in which the firm invests. Blackstone’s nearly $1.4 trillion in assets under management include global investment strategies focused on real estate, private equity, credit, infrastructure, life sciences, growth equity, secondaries and hedge funds.
