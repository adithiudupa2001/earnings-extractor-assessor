Village Farms International Reports Q3 2026 Results
Driven by Record Cannabis Revenues
•
Consolidated Net Sales Increased to $67 Million with Record Cannabis Sales of $55 Million
•
Net Income of $10 Million or $0.08 Per Share; Operating Cash Flow of $9.5 Million
•
Adjusted EBITDA from Continuing Operations of $18.2 Million or 27% of Sales
•
International Exports Increased 75% YoY to $20.8 Million in Line with Previous Quarter Record
•
Company Closes Q3 with $80 Million in Cash and Net Cash Position of $41 Million
•
Company Begins Mobilizing Efforts to Activate Texas Production Assets; Enhances Canadian Optionality by Exercising Rights to Convert 16 Acres of Delta 1 to Cannabis
VANCOUVER, British Columbia, November 9, 2026 – Village Farms International, Inc. (“Village Farms” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: VFF) today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026. All figures are in U.S. dollars unless otherwise indicated.
Management Commentary
President and Chief Executive Officer Michael DeGiglio commented, “Our third quarter results were driven by record cannabis revenues of $55 million and approximately 43% of cannabis sales from some of the world’s most attractive and fastest growing international markets. We also delivered our sixth consecutive quarter of positive net income and EPS, clearly demonstrating the strength of our sustainably profitable and expanding global cannabis enterprise.”
“In Canada, we continue to maintain our top five market share position with continued share gains in convenience product categories and a brand portfolio that remains in the top 10 of all major product categories nationally. International export sales grew 75% year-over-year, in line with last quarter’s record, and more importantly with resilient pricing relative to non-GMP compliant areas of the supply chain. As we’ve previously discussed, we continue to expect our competitive advantage with the world’s largest single-site EU-GMP certified cannabis facility will result in continued increases in demand for our compliant production capacity, and we are looking forward to our Delta 2 expansion contributing to stronger international sales growth next year along with our entrance into multiple new European jurisdictions.”
“The year-over-year variance in our margin performance in Q3 was driven primarily by a softer spot trim market in the Canadian wholesale sales channel as compared to the prior year. We believe wholesale trim volumes and pricing reached trough levels in the Canadian wholesale channel during Q3, and we expect these conditions to begin normalizing in Q4 given the visibility of our order book in October and the commencement of new supply agreements with considerably stronger contracted pricing.”
“We’re pleased to report that our Phase II Netherlands facility is now fully planted and expected to begin contributing to stronger sales growth during Q4 and throughout 2027. Our Delta 2 expansion remains on track to reach its full production capacity by the middle of next year, positioning us for continued long-term organic sales growth through calendar year 2028. Finally, we’re actively mobilizing plans to operationalize our standalone 30-acre greenhouse in Monahans, Texas for cannabis production. Monahans is the youngest, most advanced production asset in our portfolio, and we remain very focused on pursuing all potential pathways to initiate our U.S. market entry with a commencement of production in Texas.”
“As anticipated, we delivered stronger sequential cash flow performance in Q3 as capex from our Netherlands and Delta 2 expansion projects is substantially complete. We closed the quarter with nearly $80 million in cash, and believe we’re well positioned for continued profitable growth with one of the world’s most attractive and differentiated cannabis investment platforms. We continue to evaluate additional organic and acquisitive growth investments around the globe,
but will remain prudent with respect to valuation and regulatory frameworks, with a focus on profitability and long-term strategic value creation for our shareholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
(All comparable periods are for the third quarter of 2025 unless otherwise stated)
Cannabis Operations
•
Net sales increased 3% to a record of $55.0 million, from $53.5 million in the prior year period;
•
Gross margin was 48%, compared to 55% in the prior year period, with the variance driven primarily by a softer spot trim market in Canada as compared to the prior year period;
•
Net income was $7.8 million compared to $12.0 million in the prior year period, with the variance driven by lower gross margins and increased operating expenses to support future growth;
•
Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations was $14.7 million or 27% of sales, compared to $20.2 million or 38% of sales in the prior year period; and
•
Cash flow from operations was $7.3 million.
Strategic Growth and Operational Highlights
•
Maintained the Company’s top five overall market share position in the Canadian market and expanded its market share in vapes and infused pre-rolls, maintaining its top 10 position nationally in all major product categories1. The Company’s Super Toast brand also recently became the Company’s first nationally distributed brand after launching in Quebec.
•
International export sales increased 75% year-over-year to $20.8 million, in line with the previous quarter record set in Q2 of 2026. The Company remains one of the largest exporters of medical cannabis to Europe with a leading market share position in Germany.
•
The Company’s capacity expansion project at its Delta 2 greenhouse in British Columbia remains on track to be completed during Q1 of 2027. The Delta 2 expansion is expected to ramp to its full production capacity of 40 metric tonnes by mid-2027, bringing total production capacity from the Delta campus to approximately 160 metric tonnes of dried, trimmed flower annually.
•
During the third quarter the Company’s Netherlands production was temporarily reduced by propagation activities. The Groningen facility is now fully planted, and the Company continues to expect it will ramp to its maximum annualized production capacity in early 2027, positioning Village Farms Netherlands for stronger revenue growth next year.
•
The Company recently began mobilizing efforts to operationalize its standalone 30-acre (1.3 million sq. ft.) greenhouse in Monahans, Texas for cannabis production. Monahans is the youngest, most advanced production asset in the Company’s portfolio. Village Farms management remains focused on pursuing all potential pathways to initiate its U.S. market entry with a future commencement of cannabis production in Texas.
•
The Company’s Balanced Health Botanicals subsidiary continues to pursue pathways to participate in the U.S. Medicare CMMI pilot program for CBD products, and is awaiting regulatory clarity related to the pending December 11, 2026 federal hemp ban to further assess this opportunity. The Company believes it is positioned well to play a meaningful role in the future of this market if regulations preserve protections for non-synthetic, full-spectrum products.
Corporate and Other
•
On September 10, 2026, the Company announced the appointment of Hamid Shekarchi as Interim Chief Financial Officer, effective immediately. Mr. Shekarchi previously served as Chief Financial Officer of the Company’s Canadian Cannabis business and brings 18 years of financial leadership experience with a proven operational track record. The Company’s former CFO, Stephen Ruffini, was appointed to a new role as Head of Mergers & Acquisitions and will continue his role as Executive Vice President and Corporate Secretary.
•
On September 14, 2026, the Company announced the appointment of Deepak Anand as Vice President of Government Affairs. Mr. Anand will lead government relations, regulatory affairs and public policy strategies across the Company's global footprint, with a particular focus on activating new market and product growth opportunities.
•
During the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company notified Verdexa Holdings that it had elected to exercise its right to convert approximately 25% of its 60-acre (2.6 million sq. ft.) Delta 1 greenhouse in British Columbia to cannabis production. While the Company has not formalized any conversion plans for the Delta 1 greenhouse at this time, the notice provided to Verdexa provides optionality to convert 16 acres (700,000 sq. ft.) of the Delta 1 facility to cannabis production during 2027.

About Village Farms International, Inc.
Village Farms is a global leader in cannabis, plant-based consumer packaged goods, and sustainable innovation. With a legacy built on decades of Controlled Environment Agriculture expertise and Dutch farming practices, today the Company is one of the world’s largest and most profitable cannabis operators with an asset portfolio that spans over 7 million square feet of advanced greenhouse and indoor cultivation assets.
In Canada, Village Farms operates the world’s largest EU-GMP certified cannabis facility at its production campus in Delta, British Columbia, and exports products to international medical markets. The Company is also a market share leader in dried flower formats and produces and distributes some of the country’s highest quality and best-selling strains, including its flagship Pure Sunfarms Pink Kush, one of the most widely consumed strains on the planet. Village Farms’ Canadian brand portfolio includes Pure Sunfarms, Fraser Valley Weed Co., Soar, Super Toast, Pure Laine, Tam Tams and Promenade.
In the Netherlands, the Company is one of only ten licensed operators in the country’s regulated cannabis program, and in the United States its CBDistillery brand is one of the country’s premier cannabinoid wellness platforms, and it also holds equity interests in cannabis businesses in Australia and Germany. Beyond cannabis, the Company holds an equity interest in Verdexa Holdings, a private venture pursuing strategic acquisitions to build a premier branded food platform, and its Clean Energy division transforms landfill gas into renewable natural gas and receives royalties on all revenues generated.
