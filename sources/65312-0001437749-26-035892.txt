EVI Industries Reports Record First Fiscal Quarter Results, Highlighting Earnings Growth and Continued Progress Across Its Businesses
Service momentum and improved operating leverage validate, and continued customer-focused
investments extend, a disciplined long-term buy-and-build strategy
Miami, Florida – Monday, November 9, 2026 – EVI Industries, Inc. (NYSE American: EVI) announced today its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 (the quarter ended September 30, 2026), and provided updates on its operating performance, customer service and modernization initiatives, long-term buy-and-build strategy, and new consumer garment care services division. The quarter reflected progress in EVI’s efforts to improve the earnings capacity of its existing businesses while continuing to build the infrastructure and service capabilities, and develop the talent, needed to drive and support growth.
First Fiscal Quarter Financial Highlights (compared with the first quarter of fiscal 2026)
■ | Revenue increased 4.3% to a record $112.9 million;
■ | Gross profit increased 12% to a record $38.0 million, and gross margin expanded from 31.3% to a record 33.7%;
■ | Operating income increased 75% to a record $6.2 million;
■ | Net income increased 108% to a record $3.8 million, and net income margin expanded from 1.7% to 3.4% of revenues;
■ | Adjusted EBITDA increased 47% to a record $9.9 million, and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded from 6.2% to a record 8.8%;
■ | Operating cash flow increased to $7.8 million, from $1.0 million; and
■ | Net debt was $76.7 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $44.2 million at June 30, 2026.
■ | Commercial Laundry Segment:
ο | Operating income increased 38% to $11.3 million, or 10.2% of the segment’s revenues;
ο | EBITDA increased 33% to $13.4 million; and
ο | EBITDA margin expanded from 9.3% to 12.1% of the segment’s revenues, including the benefit of approximately $0.5 million of tariff refunds received during the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
Henry M. Nahmad, EVI’s Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, commented: “Our results this quarter belong to the people across EVI who answer the call when a customer’s equipment goes down, deliver the right part the first time and see complex installations through to completion. That work is why customers keep coming back, and it is increasingly showing up in our earnings. This quarter also opened a new chapter as we welcomed the Sudsies team to EVI, and I am excited about what we can build together in consumer garment care services.”
A Durable Business Built Around Long-Term Customer Relationships
Thoughtfully designed facilities, the right equipment, quality installations and well-maintained machinery are essential to the hotels, hospitals, healthcare facilities, laundromats, industrial laundries and other businesses EVI serves. Its customers depend on them every day to keep their operations running. The ongoing need to operate, maintain and ultimately replace that equipment is expected to continue to create opportunities for long-term customer relationships.
EVI’s role extends well beyond selling equipment. Its businesses advise customers on equipment selection and facility design, manage installations and complex projects, provide maintenance and repair services, and supply parts and other products used in daily operations. Management believes the breadth and national reach of these capabilities distinguish EVI from traditional equipment-focused distributors and would be difficult to replicate. This model is designed to earn customer relationships that extend beyond the initial equipment transaction.
Consolidated revenue increased 4.3% during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to the same period of the prior fiscal year, including 2.9% growth in commercial laundry revenue and approximately $1.6 million of revenue from the first month of consumer garment care operations following the acquisition of Sudsies, Inc. on September 1, 2026, which marked EVI’s entry into the consumer garment care services industry. Within commercial laundry, growth was led by parts, service and installation activities, the work that supports customers throughout the operating lives of their equipment. This more favorable revenue mix, together with meaningfully improved profitability in field service, accounted for the majority of the quarter’s increase in gross profit.
While service and installation activities led growth this quarter, equipment demand indicators remain constructive. The equipment order backlog at September 30, 2026 increased compared to June 30, 2026, and customers are increasingly selecting higher-capacity equipment, in many cases selecting fewer, larger machines to meet their capacity needs. This customer preference, together with the timing of larger projects delivered on customers’ schedules, may affect unit volumes, equipment revenue and gross margin in a given quarter. Management believes sales of higher-capacity equipment continue to deliver attractive profitability for EVI, both at the time of sale and over the life of the equipment through the ongoing parts and service needs it generates.
The Earnings Power of a Larger and More Capable Enterprise
A central objective of EVI’s modernization and operating initiatives is to convert more of its revenue and gross profit into operating earnings over time. This quarter, approximately $2.7 million of a $4.1 million increase in gross profit reached operating income, and $3.2 million reached adjusted EBITDA. EVI is achieving this operating leverage while reinvesting a portion of gross profit in recruiting and developing the next generation of leaders, sales professionals and service technicians. These investments take time to reach full productivity, moderating near-term margins but strengthening future selling capacity, service coverage, leadership depth and organic growth.
Operating Margins Today — and What Closes the Gap
Management remains focused on its previously stated long-term objective of double-digit consolidated operating margins. Progress will depend on higher productivity, a growing contribution from service and related activities, disciplined expense management and a larger earnings base. The commercial laundry segment is making meaningful progress, with segment operating margin of 10.2% and segment EBITDA margin of 12.1%. Because those results must also fund shared resources that support long-term growth, including acquisitions, technology and specialized expertise, achieving double-digit consolidated margins will require further improvement across the businesses. EVI’s modernization and optimization initiatives are designed to help EVI achieve these goals. As EVI grows, management expects those investments to be leveraged across a larger revenue base, while continued operating improvements provide an additional path to margin expansion.
Turning Scale into Advantage Through People, Processes, and Technology
EVI is investing in people, processes and technology to increase productivity and elevate the customer experience. Better service can win new customers and keeps existing ones coming back. Commercial laundry operators depend on quick answers, accurate parts and dependable technicians because downtime disrupts their operations and their customers.
EVI has built what management believes is a distinctive combination in its industry: a national field service network supported by modern enterprise systems. During the quarter, EVI’s field service operations completed approximately 31,000 appointments for nearly 12,000 unique customers, using substantially deployed enterprise resource planning, field service and business intelligence systems. With that infrastructure in place, the focus has shifted to improving scheduling and access to parts and service information, bringing greater consistency to everyday processes and equipping technicians and operating teams to serve customers more efficiently.
Service revenue increased and service gross profit improved significantly compared with the prior-year quarter, an encouraging indicator of progress across the service business. Management believes EVI’s model, which pairs local customer relationships and decision-making with shared technology, training and analytical capabilities, is difficult to replicate in a fragmented industry where many successful local companies lack the resources to build those capabilities on their own. As those capabilities are adopted across a growing enterprise, management expects them to support further productivity gains, new customer relationships and repeat business.
Buy-and-Build Strategy Drives Long-Term Development
Since 2016, EVI has pursued a buy-and-build strategy based on partnering with established businesses led by people with deep industry knowledge and customer relationships. The objective is not to complete the greatest number of acquisitions in a given quarter. It is to find businesses that fit the Company’s culture and operating philosophy, invest responsibly, preserve entrepreneurial decision-making, and support their development.
Once a company joins EVI, much of the work is just beginning. Developing talent, expanding service capabilities, improving customer processes, enhancing procurement and deploying technology require consistency and patience, and the strongest benefits are often not visible in an acquired company’s first several quarters. EVI believes the cumulative impact of these efforts can create value both within each business and across the organization.
Following the end of the quarter, EVI acquired West Palm Beach, Florida-based Commercial Laundry Equipment (“CLZ”), a second-generation, family-owned distributor of commercial laundry products and provider of installation and maintenance services. CLZ expands EVI’s presence in Florida and adds an experienced team of 18 employees with longstanding customer relationships.
EVI continues to explore potential acquisitions in both commercial laundry and consumer garment care services. Management remains selective, evaluating each opportunity for business quality, leadership, cultural fit, capital requirements and potential for long-term development. EVI believes returns from thoughtfully developing the businesses it already owns are as important as the pace of future acquisitions.
Building a Second Growth Platform: Consumer Garment Care Services
On September 1, 2026, EVI completed its acquisition of Sudsies, establishing a consumer garment care services division and extending the Company’s long-term model into a complementary area of the laundry industry. Sudsies provides an established operating foundation with a focus on quality, convenience and customer service.
The consumer garment care market is fragmented and characterized by recurring customer needs. EVI sees an opportunity to apply principles that have guided its commercial laundry strategy: partner with capable operators, maintain a service-oriented culture, and invest in people, processes and technology to support sustainable development. Although the end customer and service model differ from commercial laundry distribution, management believes the same commitment to operating excellence, customer loyalty and disciplined capital allocation is relevant.
The Company intends to develop this business deliberately. Its priorities include supporting the Sudsies team, learning from its operating model, maintaining a high standard of customer service and evaluating opportunities for thoughtful expansion. Management believes a strong foundation and disciplined integration are more important than rapidly increasing the size of the new division. Results in the division’s first month also reflected seasonal patterns in the business and investments EVI is making in back-office personnel and support functions to prepare for future acquisitions, and management expects these investments to be absorbed over time as the division scales.
Cash Flow, Financial Strength, and Capital Allocation
The Company generated approximately $7.8 million of cash from operating activities during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with approximately $1.0 million in the prior-year period. Operating cash generation helps provide financial flexibility as EVI invests in its existing operations, supports customers and evaluates further acquisitions.
Net debt was approximately $76.7 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $44.2 million at June 30, 2026. The approximately $32.5 million increase primarily reflected borrowings in connection with the Sudsies acquisition, partially offset by operating cash generated during the quarter. Management believes EVI maintains ample liquidity, including availability under its revolving credit facility, to pursue acquisition opportunities and, to the extent deemed necessary or advisable, may seek additional capital resources to fund its strategic growth plans, including acquisitions and other strategic transactions. EVI remains focused on balancing these opportunities with prudent leverage and financial flexibility.
Tariff Impacts and the Operating Environment
EVI continues to monitor trade policy, tariffs, supplier costs and other conditions that can affect equipment pricing, product availability and customer purchasing decisions. The Company works with its suppliers and customers to manage these changes while maintaining service levels and the competitiveness of its offerings. Management believes the essential nature of the products and services EVI provides, its relationships with established manufacturers and its broad customer base will continue to support the business over time, while acknowledging that cost pressures and project timing may affect results in individual periods. During the first quarter of fiscal 2027, EVI received approximately $0.5 million of refunds of previously paid tariffs, which were recorded as a reduction to cost of sales.
Looking Ahead
Mr. Nahmad added: “Our strategy requires patience because creating a better business takes more than capital. It takes trusted relationships, capable people, good systems and consistent execution. We have invested in those foundations through periods when the full financial benefit was not yet visible. This quarter is encouraging, but we are not declaring the work finished. We will continue to invest with discipline, improve the experience we provide to customers and pursue acquisitions when we believe they can create value. We measure our progress over years, not by the number of transactions or the results of any single quarter.”

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Information
In this press release, EVI discloses the non-GAAP financial measures of adjusted EBITDA and segment EBITDA. EVI defines adjusted EBITDA as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization, and stock compensation. Adjusted EBITDA is determined by adding interest expense, income taxes, depreciation, amortization, and stock compensation to net income, as shown in the attached statement of Condensed Consolidated Earnings before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization, and Stock Compensation. EVI defines segment EBITDA as segment operating income plus segment depreciation and amortization. Segment operating income excludes stock compensation and unallocated corporate expenses, which are presented separately in the attached segment reconciliation tables. Commercial laundry segment EBITDA refers to the segment EBITDA of EVI’s commercial laundry segment.
EVI considers adjusted EBITDA and segment EBITDA to be important indicators of its operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA is also used by companies, lenders, investors and others because it excludes certain items that can vary widely across different industries or among companies within the same industry. For example, interest expense can be dependent on a company’s capital structure, debt levels and credit ratings, and the tax positions of companies can vary because of their differing abilities to take advantage of tax benefits and because of the tax policies of the jurisdictions in which they operate. EVI believes segment EBITDA assists investors in evaluating the operating performance of each of its segments between periods.
Reconciliations of adjusted EBITDA to net income, the most directly comparable financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP, and of segment EBITDA to segment operating income, the most directly comparable financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP, are included in the financial information accompanying this press release. Adjusted EBITDA and segment EBITDA should not be considered as alternatives to net income, operating income or any other measure of financial performance or liquidity, including cash flow, derived in accordance with GAAP, or to any other method of analyzing EVI’s results as reported under GAAP. These measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.
About EVI Industries
EVI Industries, Inc. (NYSE American: EVI), through its subsidiaries, operates in the commercial laundry distribution and consumer garment care services industries. EVI’s commercial laundry businesses sell or lease equipment, supply parts and accessories, and provide design, installation, maintenance, repair and related services to commercial, industrial, institutional, governmental and retail customers. EVI’s consumer garment care operations provide cleaning, pressing, alteration, restoration and related services for apparel and household textiles through retail and pickup-and-delivery operations. The Company pursues a long-term buy-and-build strategy focused on developing businesses, investing in people and capabilities, and creating shareholder value.
