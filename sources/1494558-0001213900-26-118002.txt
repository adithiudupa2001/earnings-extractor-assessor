CUPERTINO, Calif., November 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Ambow Education Holding Ltd. (“Ambow” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: AMBO), an AI-driven technology company delivering phygital intelligence solutions for education, enterprise collaboration and live events, today announced its unaudited financial and operating results for the three-month and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter and First Nine Months 2026 and Recent Financial and Operating Highlights
Growth
● | Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 increased 21.4% year over year to $1.7 million, with both business lines contributing. Educational programs and services revenue increased to $1.3 million from $1.1 million, and HybriU licensing and sales revenue increased to $0.4 million from $0.3 million.
● | Net revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased 6.2% year over year to $6.9 million, led by educational programs and services, which increased to $5.3 million from $5.0 million.
Subsequent Event
● | Subsequent to quarter end, Ambow announced that HybriU will exhibit at CES 2027, January 6 through 9, 2027, at Booth #10308 in the Las Vegas Convention Center North Hall, where the Company will demonstrate a working phygital classroom, meeting room and event environment to the channel partners, integrators, enterprises and institutions that deploy and use HybriU.
“Both of our business lines contributed to revenue growth in the third quarter, reflecting continued progress in our education services and technology businesses,” said Dr. Jin Huang, Chief Executive Officer and Chair of the Board of Ambow Education Holding Ltd.
“During the quarter, we increased combined selling and marketing and research and development expenses by $0.2 million to support the commercial launches of HybriU Events On Demand and the AI-Driven Adaptive Course Generator, further strengthen HybriU’s positioning as Digital Intelligent Infrastructure, and prepare for CES 2027 in January.
These strategic investments were supported by continued improvements in operating efficiency. Year to date, net cash used in operating activities decreased by approximately 50% compared with the same period last year. General and administrative expenses also declined again during the quarter. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $6.1 million in cash, restricted cash and short-term investments.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 increased by 21.4% to $1.7 million from $1.4 million for the same period of 2025. Educational programs and services revenue increased to $1.3 million from $1.1 million, and HybriU licensing and sales revenue increased to $0.4 million from $0.3 million.
Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.5 million, compared with $0.5 million for the same period of 2025. Gross profit margin was 30.6% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 35.7% for the third quarter of 2025.
Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 increased by 18.2% to $1.3 million from $1.1 million for the same period of 2025. The increase was attributable to growth investment: selling and marketing expenses increased to $0.4 million from $0.3 million and research and development expenses increased to $0.2 million from $0.1 million, a combined increase of $196 thousand, while general and administrative expenses decreased by $40 thousand to $0.7 million.
Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.8 million, compared with an operating loss of $0.6 million for the same period of 2025. The $148 thousand increase in operating loss reflected the $196 thousand increase in combined selling and marketing and research and development expenses, partially offset by the reduction in general and administrative expenses.
Net loss attributable to ordinary shareholders for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.8 million, or $0.27 per basic and diluted ADS, compared with a net loss of $0.6 million, or $0.21 per basic and diluted ADS, for the same period of 2025.
First Nine Months 2026 Financial Results
Net revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased by 6.2% to $6.9 million from $6.5 million for the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily due to net revenues generated by educational programs and services, which increased to $5.3 million from $5.0 million. HybriU licensing and sales revenue was $1.5 million in both periods.
Gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 increased by 3.0% to $3.4 million from $3.3 million for the same period of 2025. Gross profit margin was 50.0% for the first nine months of 2026, compared with 51.1% for the same period of 2025. The change in gross margin was mainly attributable to an increase in academic costs associated with the delivery of educational programs, which exceeded the growth in revenue during the period.

Operating expenses for the first nine months of 2026 increased by 11.4% to $3.9 million from $3.5 million for the same period of 2025. Selling and marketing expenses increased to $1.0 million from $0.8 million and research and development expenses increased to $0.6 million from $0.3 million, a combined increase of $489 thousand directed to HybriU product launches, go-to-market programs and NewSchool enrollment. General and administrative expenses decreased 8.7% to $2.1 million from $2.3 million. Operating expenses for 2026 also included a $161 thousand non-cash impairment loss on construction in progress recognized in the second quarter.
Operating loss for the first nine months of 2026 was $0.5 million, compared with an operating loss of $0.2 million for the same period of 2025. The $301 thousand increase in operating loss reflected the $489 thousand increase in combined selling and marketing and research and development expenses and the $161 thousand impairment loss, partially offset by a $236 thousand reduction in general and administrative expenses and a $113 thousand increase in gross profit.
Net loss attributable to ordinary shareholders for the first nine months of 2026 was $0.5 million, or $0.17 per basic and diluted ADS, compared with net income of $1.3 million, or $0.44 per basic and diluted ADS, for the same period of 2025. Net income for the first nine months of 2025 included a one-time gain on lease settlement of $1.5 million recognized in the second quarter of 2025.
Balance Sheet and Liquidity
As of September 30, 2026, Ambow maintained cash resources of $6.1 million, comprising cash and cash equivalents of $2.4 million, restricted cash of $2.7 million and short-term investments of $1.0 million in time deposits. Cash and cash equivalents increased from $0.8 million as of December 31, 2025, as $4.6 million of restricted cash was released during the period. As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s consolidated current assets exceeded its consolidated current liabilities by $3.9 million, and consolidated net assets were $7.9 million.
Total liabilities decreased to $10.4 million as of September 30, 2026 from $12.5 million as of December 31, 2025, including a reduction in accounts payable to $0.9 million from $1.6 million, while borrowings were unchanged at $3.2 million. Net cash used in operating activities was $0.7 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with $1.4 million for the same period of 2025.
The Company’s financial and operating results for the third quarter and first nine months of 2026 are also available in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) at www.sec.gov.
About Ambow Education Holding Ltd.
Ambow Education Holding Ltd. (NYSE American: AMBO) is an AI-driven technology company delivering phygital intelligence solutions for education, enterprise collaboration and live events.
Founded in 2000 and headquartered in Cupertino, California, Ambow develops technologies that connect physical environments, artificial intelligence, digital systems, workflows and institutional knowledge.
Through HybriU, Ambow enables institutions and professionals to capture, organize, activate and reuse knowledge generated across classrooms, meetings and events, creating intelligence assets that can become more valuable over time.
For more information, visit www.ambow.com and www.hybriu.com.
